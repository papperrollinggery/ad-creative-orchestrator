# Do Not Copy

## Source Rules

```text
Do not treat UGC as official source.
Do not copy brand marks, product design, talent, composition, or exact storyline.
Do not use reference assets as client assets unless rights are confirmed.
```

## Reference-specific Restrictions

| Reference ID | Restriction | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| REF-QLO-001 | Do not copy story arc or talent styling | Use only rhythm and transition logic |
| REF-QLO-002 | Do not copy functional claims | Product claims require client-approved wording |
| REF-QLO-003 | Do not cite UGC as official evidence | UGC is cultural context only |
| REF-QLO-004 | Do not copy competitor brand codes | Category baseline only |
| REF-QLO-005 | Do not invent product details | Product macro must match real product assets |
